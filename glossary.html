About this glossary

This glossary collects the English technical terms from each lecture and gives each one in five more languages, so you can follow the vocabulary of the course whichever language you think in. Terms are grouped by the lecture where they appear, and a term that recurs in a later lecture is listed again there. The last column tells you where in the lecture each term is used.

The page works offline once it has opened — nothing is loaded from the internet. Choose a lecture and a language below and only those terms are shown, so you are never faced with the whole six-language table at once.
